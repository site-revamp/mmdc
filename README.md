# MMDC Medical & Dental Center — Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

MMDC Medical & Dental Center is a multidisciplinary dental clinic located in Barsha Heights (TECOM), Dubai, offering general, cosmetic, restorative, orthodontic, pediatric and specialist dental treatments.

The clinic serves individuals and families seeking comprehensive dental care, with a positioning built around North American-trained dental professionals, multidisciplinary expertise and personalised patient care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating these strengths more effectively, helping prospective patients discover relevant treatments, understand the clinical team's expertise and take the next step towards booking an appointment.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 01 — Clearer and More Differentiated Positioning

**Previous issue:** The original homepage headline, "Best Dental Clinic in Dubai", was generic and did not immediately communicate what distinguishes MMDC from competing dental clinics.

**Implemented improvement:** The new homepage places greater emphasis on North American-trained professionals, multidisciplinary expertise and personalised dental care. This establishes a more distinctive positioning and communicates the clinic's value proposition more clearly.

### 02 — More Intuitive Treatment Discovery

**Previous issue:** The original homepage presented broad treatment categories without sufficiently connecting patient needs, individual treatments and relevant specialist disciplines.

**Implemented improvement:** The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured treatment discovery experience, making it easier for visitors to explore the clinic's dental services and understand its different specialist treatment areas.

### 03 — Stronger Presentation of Clinical Expertise and Credibility

**Previous issue:** Information about the clinical team, international training, dental technology and patient reviews was distributed across the existing website rather than presented as a cohesive demonstration of clinical credibility.

**Implemented improvement:** The new homepage gives greater prominence to clinical expertise and credibility, bringing together the clinical team, professional specialisms, verified qualifications, patient feedback and relevant clinical capabilities.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured and cohesive presentation of MMDC's services and clinical expertise.

Key design improvements include:

- **Visual hierarchy:** Clearer prioritisation of the main positioning, treatment offerings and clinical expertise.
- **Content organisation:** A more logical progression from introducing the clinic to exploring treatments and understanding its clinical team.
- **Treatment discovery:** A more accessible presentation of dental services and specialist treatment areas.
- **Clinical credibility:** A more cohesive presentation of the professionals, specialisms and supporting trust elements.
- **User experience:** Clearer navigation through the clinic's core information and treatment offerings.
- **Conversion opportunities:** Greater clarity around appointment enquiries, telephone contact and WhatsApp communication.
- **Overall consistency:** A more unified presentation of the clinic's positioning, services and professional identity.
